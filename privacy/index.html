1. What this website collects

No form, account registration, online booking submission, waitlist, payment flow, or site data-submission endpoint appears in this repository. The appointment buttons scroll to the barber list; the barber links are example addresses and the page shows a “not available yet” message instead of submitting or confirming a booking. The contact details and review link shown in the source were placeholders and are now marked as pending, not offered as working contact links.

When your browser requests a page or asset, the web host necessarily receives a network request. Depending on its configuration, the host or its infrastructure may process technical information such as your IP address, requested URL, date and time, browser headers, and error details. The actual host, exact log fields, recipients, and retention practices could not be confirmed from this repository.

1. Información que recopila este sitio

En este repositorio no se encontró ningún formulario, registro de cuentas, envío de reservas en línea, lista de espera, flujo de pagos ni endpoint para enviar datos del sitio. Los botones de reserva desplazan a la lista de barberos; los enlaces de reserva son direcciones de ejemplo y la página muestra un aviso de que la función aún no está disponible, en lugar de enviar o confirmar una reserva. Los datos de contacto y el enlace de reseñas del código eran marcadores y ahora aparecen como pendientes, no como enlaces de contacto funcionales.

2. How information is used and shared

The site uses its content and browser-side code to display barber, service, hours, and location information and to remember the language and theme you choose. No analytics, advertising pixel, marketing automation, customer database, authentication, or messaging integration was found in the published site code.

The barber photographs and fonts used by the site are stored with the website rather than requested from remote font or image CDNs. Google Maps is not loaded automatically: the home page displays a notice and loads the map only if you select its load button. A separate Google Maps link also opens only when selected. When you choose either map action, Google may receive technical request information such as your IP address and browser headers and may process it under its own terms and privacy notice. The site does not control Google's subsequent processing or retention. The regions where Google processes data were not verified.

No visitor-submitted information is sent to a booking, email, SMS, payment, or database provider by the site as currently implemented. A future email or phone contact channel, if the owner confirms and publishes one, would involve the visitor's chosen email or telephone provider and should be described after its actual configuration is known.

Un enlace independiente a Google Maps también se abre únicamente cuando lo seleccionas. Al elegir cualquiera de esas opciones, Google puede recibir información técnica de la solicitud, como la dirección IP y encabezados del navegador, y procesarla conforme a sus propios términos y aviso de privacidad. 3. Local storage, cookies, and tracking

The site stores two preferences in your browser's local storage: siteLanguage (en or es) and siteTheme (light or dark). These values are used only to apply your language and appearance choices on pages of this site; the site code does not send them to a server. They remain in that browser until changed or cleared. If local storage is unavailable, the site uses its default English and dark theme for that visit.

No site-set cookie, analytics script, advertising pixel, or other tracking script was found in the repository. The map is a third-party request only if you choose to load it or open Google Maps; Google's own behavior is outside the site's control. You can decline to load the embedded map or block that request in your browser.
